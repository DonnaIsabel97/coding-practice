## 💻 LeetCode & UMPIRE

LeetCode problems are used to practice data structures, algorithms, problem-solving patterns, and technical interview communication.

Rather than focusing only on reaching the correct solution, I use the **UMPIRE problem-solving approach**:

### U — Understand

Clarify the problem, inputs, outputs, constraints, assumptions, and edge cases.

### M — Match

Identify data structures, algorithms, or known problem-solving patterns that could be appropriate for the problem.

### P — Plan

Develop a clear step-by-step approach before implementing the solution.

### I — Implement

Translate the plan into clean and readable code.

### R — Review

Walk through the solution, test it against different cases, and identify potential errors or missing edge cases.

### E — Evaluate

Analyze the time and space complexity of the solution and consider possible optimizations or alternative approaches.

The goal is to develop a **repeatable problem-solving process** that can be communicated clearly during technical interviews rather than simply memorizing solutions.
